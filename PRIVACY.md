# Privacy

Wholeframe does not collect, transmit, or sell data.

- Capture runs only after you click the toolbar button, use the keyboard shortcut, or choose the context-menu command.
- The extension reads the active tab's layout (scroll size, sticky elements) in order to take the screenshot. It does not read form fields for its own use, and it does not send page contents anywhere.
- The latest screenshot is stored locally in the extension's IndexedDB so the preview tab can show it. Downloaded files go to your normal downloads folder.
- Settings (delay, sticky-header option, lazy-load option) are stored in `chrome.storage.local` on your machine.
- There is no account, no analytics, and no remote server.
